EXAMPLES

example_onion: Example graph that illustrates onion decomposition. From Hébert-Dufresne, Grochow, Allard. (2016). Multi-scale structure and topological anomaly detection via a new network statistic: The onion decomposition. Scientific Reports, 6(1), 31708.

barbell_multidensity: Two seven-node cliques joined by a path of four nodes.

les_miserables_multidensity: Two mirror copies of Les Misérables joined at the most peripheric node by a new edge; nodes are characters, and an edge joins two who appear in the same scene.

game_thrones_multidensity: Two mirror copies of Game of Thrones joined at the most peripheric node by a new edge; nodes are characters, and an edge joins two named near each other in the text.

tree_binomial: The binomial tree B7; nodes are vertices, and an edge joins a parent to its child.


SPHERE

fullerene_structures_C1500: A molecule of 1500 atoms; nodes are carbon atoms, and an edge is a bond.

fullerene_structures_C3840: A molecule of 3840 atoms; nodes are carbon atoms, and an edge is a bond.

icosphere_2562: A subdivided icosahedron; nodes are mesh vertices, and an edge is a side of a triangle.

icosphere_6252: A subdivided icosahedron; nodes are mesh vertices, and an edge is a side of a triangle.


FLAT

lattice_squares_4096: A square tiling; nodes are the corners of the squares, and edges are the sides.

lattice_monotiles_5992: A disc of the hat aperiodic monotile tiling; nodes are tile corners, and an edge is a tile side. From Smith, Myers, Kaplan, Goodman-Strauss. (2023). An aperiodic monotile. Arxiv preprint.

lattice_penrose_5981: A disc of a Penrose tiling; nodes are tile corners, and an edge is a tile side.

fibonacci_sunflower: Points on a Fibonacci spiral, as seeds sit in a sunflower; an edge joins neighbouring points.


GEOMETRIC

spot: A toy cow mesh; nodes are mesh vertices, and an edge is a side of a triangle. From Keenan Crane's 3D Model Repository (cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/ModelRepository) 

stanford_bunny: A scanned bunny mesh; nodes are mesh vertices, and an edge is a side of a triangle. From Stanford 3D Scanning Repository (graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep).

torus_2560: A triangulated torus, 80 by 32; nodes are mesh vertices, and an edge is a side of a triangle.

bob: A toy duck mesh; nodes are mesh vertices, and an edge is a side of a triangle. From Keenan Crane's 3D Model Repository (cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/ModelRepository) 

cylinder_2560: A square grid wrapped into an open tube, 80 by 32; nodes are grid points, and an edge is a grid side.

oloid: An oloid mesh; nodes are mesh vertices, and an edge is a side of a triangle. From Keenan Crane's 3D Model Repository (cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/ModelRepository) 


FRACTAL

sierpinski_triangle_3282: The Sierpiński triangle; nodes are the corners of its smallest triangles, and an edge is a side.

sierpinski_square_5280: The Sierpiński carpet, level 4; nodes are the corners of its smallest squares, and an edge is a side.

sierpinski_tetrahedron_2050: The Sierpiński tetrahedron; nodes are the corners of its smallest tetrahedra, and an edge is a side.

sierpinski_cube_5328: The Menger sponge, level 2; nodes are the corners of its smallest cubes, and an edge is a side.

dragon_curve_4961: The Heighway dragon, folded 13 times; nodes are the points the curve passes through, and an edge is one of its steps.


LITERARY

les_miserables: Nodes are characters in Hugo's novel; an edge joins two who appear in the same scene.

game_thrones: Nodes are characters in A Storm of Swords; an edge joins two named near each other in the text.

star_wars: Nodes are characters in Episodes I to VII; an edge joins two who speak in the same scene.


LANGUAGE

indo_european: Nodes are Indo-European languages and the subgroups they belong to; an edge joins a subgroup to a member.

word_ladder: Nodes are five-letter English words; an edge joins two that differ in one letter.


WIKIPEDIA

wiki_science: Nodes are Wikipedia articles on academic fields; an edge joins two with similar text.

thinkers: Nodes are thinkers on Wikipedia; an edge joins two when one influenced the other.

mathematicians: Nodes are scholars on Wikipedia, at least one of each pair a mathematician; an edge joins an advisor and a student.

programming_languages: Nodes are programming languages on Wikipedia; an edge joins two when one influenced the other.

music_genres: Nodes are music genres on Wikipedia; an edge joins a genre to one it came from, a subgenre or a fusion.

jazz_musicians: Nodes are jazz musicians and bands on Wikipedia; an edge joins two listed as associated acts.

amazon: Nodes are rivers of the Amazon basin on Wikipedia; an edge joins a river to the one it flows into.


SOCIAL

karate_club: Nodes are members of a university karate club; an edge joins two who met outside the club. The labels are the two factions it split into.

netscience: Nodes are network scientists; an edge joins two who wrote a paper together.

dolphins: Nodes are bottlenose dolphins of Doubtful Sound; an edge joins two often seen together.


BIOLOGICAL

plant_pollinator_kato: Nodes are plants and insects in a Kyoto beech forest; an edge joins an insect to a plant it visits.

plant_pollinator_robertson: Nodes are plants and insects in Illinois; an edge joins an insect to a plant it visits.

green_fluorescent_protein: Nodes are the residues of the jellyfish protein; an edge joins two whose alpha carbons lie within 8 Å.

hemoglobin: Nodes are the residues of human hemoglobin's four chains; an edge joins two whose alpha carbons lie within 8 Å.

groel_ring: Nodes are the residues of one seven-fold ring of a bacterial chaperonin; an edge joins two whose alpha carbons lie within 8 Å.

celegans_male_gap_junction: Nodes are neurons of the male nematode; an edge is a gap junction between two.

human_connectome: Nodes are brain regions; an edge joins two linked by fibre tracts, averaged over many people. From braingraph.org, averaged over OASIS-3 participants. Data were provided in part by OASIS-3: Principal Investigators: T. Benzinger, D. Marcus, J. Morris; NIH P30 AG066444, P50 AG00561, P30 NS09857781, P01 AG026276, P01 AG003991, R01 AG043434, UL1 TR000448, R01 EB009352.


INFRASTRUCTURE

power: Nodes are generators, transformers and substations of the Western US grid; an edge is a power line.

european_power_grid: Nodes are European substations at 330 kV and above, at geographic positions; an edge is a line between two.

internet_top_pop: Nodes are backbone points of presence of a Midwest fiber network, at geographic positions; an edge is a link between two.


TRANSPORTATION

london_transport: Nodes are Tube, Overground and DLR stations, at geographic positions; an edge joins two stations next to each other on a line.

urban_streets_ahmedabad: Nodes are street intersections in a square mile of Ahmedabad, at geographic positions; an edge is a street segment.

urban_streets_savannah: Nodes are street intersections in a square mile of Savannah, at geographic positions; an edge is a street segment. From Crucitti, Latora, Porta. (2006). Centrality measures in spatial networks of urban streets. Physics Review 73, 036125.

eu_airlines: Nodes are European airports, at geographic positions; an edge joins two with a direct flight on any airline.
